# Sequence-Determinants-of-Nucleosome-Formation
Codes associated with the PhD dissertation Sequence Determinants of Nucleosome Formation, By Aditi Biswas, Durham University, UK.

All codes are properly commented. Filenames are descriptive and also point to the associated figure they codes relate to.

Calculation of intrinsic cyclisability according to the Park et al method requires previously published code: https://github.com/codergirl1106/Cyclizability-Prediction-Website/

Codes 1-23 pertain to chapter 2
Codes 24-38 pertain to chapter 4
Codes 39-44 pertain to chapter 5.
